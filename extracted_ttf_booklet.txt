ABOUT THE COMPETITION
The TechnoSchool ICT & Robotics Competition 2026 is a two‑stage competition designed
for students from Grades 1 to 12.
The competition provides students with opportunities to develop their skills in꞉
 ICT
 Coding
 Robotics
 Artificial Intelligence
 Cybersecurity
 Innovation
Students will first participate through their respective schools. The school will conduct the
preliminary evaluation and identify the students who will represent the school at the Grand
Finale.
The competition will be conducted in two stages꞉
STAGE 1 — SCHOOL‑LEVEL COMPETITION
Students participate in the preliminary competition through their school.
Teachers/school coordinators will꞉
1. Register participating students.
2. Guide students on the assigned competition topics.
3. Conduct or coordinate the school‑level evaluation.
4. Identify the top‑performing students in each category.
5. Submit the selected finalists to TechnoSchool.
STAGE 2 — GRAND FINALE
The selected students will represent their school at the TechnoSchool ICT & Robotics
Competition Grand Finale.
